QuickSolve business launch checklist
Replace the bracketed notes with your own facts. This is not legal or tax advice.

Offer
- Write the job you will do in one sentence.
- Name who it is for, and who it is not for.
- List what is included and what is billed extra.
- Set a price and a revision limit.

Setup
- Choose a business name you can also use as a domain.
- Open a separate bank or wallet for the work.
- Decide how you will invoice (QuickSolve invoice generator is one option).
- Write a short privacy note if you collect emails.

Store or freelance launch week
- Publish the offer page.
- Send the offer to five people who already know your work.
- Track the first ten enquiries in a simple sheet.
- Review what you charged after the first paid job.

Weekly review
- Money in, money out, hours worked.
- One change you will make next week.
