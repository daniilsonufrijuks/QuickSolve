Freelance Business Starter Kit
QuickSolve sample download

This kit is a working checklist you can copy into your own notes. It is not legal advice and it does not create a contract for you.

1. Business basics
- Trading name
- Contact email you actually check
- Address you are willing to put on invoices
- Currency you will bill in

2. First offer
- One service you can deliver this month
- What the client receives
- What you need from the client before you start
- Price and when it is due

3. Invoice habits
- Send the invoice when the work is delivered, not weeks later
- Put the due date on the invoice
- Keep a copy of every invoice you send
- Record the payment date when it arrives

4. Simple weekly review
- What was delivered
- What is waiting on the client
- What is unpaid
- What you will not take on next week

5. Files in this kit
- This checklist
- A reminder that QuickSolve's invoice generator creates documents only. It does not collect the payment.

Replace every line with your own details before you send anything to a client.
